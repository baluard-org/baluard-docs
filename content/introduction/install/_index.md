Baluard ships as system packages for Linux, an installer for Windows, and an app zip for macOS. This section covers the install steps for each platform and the two download warnings you may meet: SmartScreen on Windows and Gatekeeper on macOS.

1. [Installation](@/introduction/install/installation.md): package repositories, direct downloads, and the CLI.
2. [The Windows SmartScreen warning](@/introduction/install/windows-smartscreen.md): why Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" and how to continue.
3. [The macOS Gatekeeper warning](@/introduction/install/macos-gatekeeper.md): why macOS says it cannot verify the app and how to open it anyway.
