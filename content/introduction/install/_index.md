Baluard ships as system packages for Linux, an installer for Windows, and an app zip for macOS. This section covers the install steps for each platform and the notes you may meet along the way: the Linux AppImage, SmartScreen on Windows, and Gatekeeper on macOS.

1. [Installation](@/introduction/install/installation.md): package repositories, direct downloads, and the CLI.
2. [The Linux AppImage](@/introduction/install/linux-appimage.md): FUSE, app menus with AppManager or Gear Lever, updates, and immutable distributions.
3. [The Windows SmartScreen warning](@/introduction/install/windows-smartscreen.md): why Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" and how to continue.
4. [The macOS Gatekeeper warning](@/introduction/install/macos-gatekeeper.md): why macOS says it cannot verify the app and how to open it anyway.
