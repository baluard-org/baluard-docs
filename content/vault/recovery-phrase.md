When you create a new vault in Baluard, the app generates a **24-word recovery phrase** (for example: *apple, river, lantern, ...*). Those 24 words are the master key to your vault in an emergency. Store them safely, and keep them forever.

## Why it matters

Baluard has no central server, no user accounts, and no "Forgot password" button. Nobody at Baluard can reset your access or recover your data for you.

If your laptop is stolen, your phone breaks, or you lose access to all of your devices at once, your 24-word recovery phrase is the **only** way to get back into your vault. Without it, your encrypted data is permanently locked.

## Your second factor (2FA)

In Baluard, your vault is protected by two things:

1. **Your master password** (something you know).
2. **Your device key** (something your device holds).

Knowing your master password alone is not enough to open your vault on a brand-new device. To add a new device or recover after a disaster, you need either an existing enrolled device **or** your 24-word recovery phrase. In practice, those 24 words act as your **two-factor authentication (2FA)** for catastrophic recovery.

## How to store it safely, forever

Treat your 24-word phrase like the deed to your house or a spare key to a safe:

- **Write it down on paper.** Use a pen, write clearly, and double-check the spelling and order of all 24 words.
- **Keep it somewhere physical and safe.** A locked drawer, a fireproof home safe, or a bank safety deposit box.
- **Consider a second physical copy.** If a fire or flood hits your home, a second copy stored with a trusted family member or in another location saves you.
- **Keep it offline.** Never take a photo or screenshot of the words. Never save them in an email, a cloud note, a chat message, or a plain text file on your computer.
- **Never share it.** Anyone who gets both your master password and your 24-word phrase can unlock your vault.

## When you will need it

You do not need the 24-word phrase for everyday use—your devices unlock with your master password. You will only need it when:

- All of your enrolled devices are lost, broken, or wiped at the same time.
- You are setting up a new device and do not have an existing device nearby to approve it.

Keep it safe, keep it offline, and never throw it away.
